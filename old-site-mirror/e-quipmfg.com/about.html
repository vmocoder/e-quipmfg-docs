We have been in the food processing and meat packaging industry for over 35 years since 1976.  Our humble beginnings brought us to a firm faith in customer service and satisfaction.   Our customers get the advantage of hard work and trustworthy service.  E-Quip’s products make production easier and more efficient for our clients. There is nothing that makes us happier than when our products help a company reach their potential.

 

E-Quip Manufacturing is an industry leader in custom stainless steel food processing equipment such as conveyors, mixers, dumpers, blenders, spiral freezers, tunnel freezers and platforms.  If you would like to see more pictures or videos of our equipment please visit our website at www.e-quipmfg.com.

 

We offer a wide range of processing equipment to help improve productivity and streamline the process in your plant.  E-Quip can supply one piece of equipment, or provide a complete turn-key system.  “Keeping all your design requirements in mind”

 

All of our products are designed and manufactured with the highest quality and hygiene in mind.  Our products meet all USDA approval.

 

If you have an application or processing need, we would like the opportunity to earn your business and facilitate your next project.
